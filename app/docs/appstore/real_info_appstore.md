Think it, capture it. Say it, get it done.

Spur is the companion app for an AI recording ring—an always-on voice entry on your hand. Capture inspiration and important conversations anytime, then automatically transcribe, organize, and summarize. Use voice to control your AI Agent and turn notes into action.

WHAT IT IS
• A wearable way to capture ideas the moment they appear
• An always-on recording entry you don’t have to dig for
• An AI tool that organizes meetings and key conversations
• A personal hardware entry for AI Agents
• A bridge from ideas and content to real action

WHAT IT IS NOT
• A hidden listening device
• A professional interview recorder
• A traditional health ring
• A Bluetooth earbud in ring form
• An always-on 24/7 life-recording device
• An on-device large-model computer

CORE LOOPS
1) Inspiration → instant capture → AI organization → plan → AI Agent execution
2) Meeting → record & transcribe → notes → extract tasks → AI Agent execution

IN THE APP
• Fast capture: one-tap recording from the ring—less unlocking and hunting for apps
• Auto organize: transcription, summary, categorization, and tags—less replaying and cleanup
• Scene results: turn recordings into drafts, to-dos, and meeting notes for creators and work
• Agent control: connect tools like Codex / OpenClaw and use the ring as a hardware entry for Agents
• Reminder loop: create schedules, alarms, and reminders by voice

BUILT FOR
Creators, founders, PMs, designers, sales, advisors, lawyers, journalists, researchers—and anyone who gets ideas on the move or works with a personal AI agent.

Privacy matters: recording state is clear and visible. We do not offer a fully hidden recording mode.
