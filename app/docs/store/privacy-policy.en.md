This Privacy Policy explains how the Spur app (“Spur”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Spur mobile application and related cloud services.

Spur is a companion app for a recording ring. It helps you sync recordings from the ring to your iPhone, view transcripts and summaries, and use AI features when you choose to process audio.

By using Spur, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use AI upload features and uninstall the app if you prefer not to continue.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Audio recordings
- Recordings may be created on your recording ring and synced to your iPhone over Bluetooth.
- You may also create or store related audio files on the phone.
- By default, synced recordings stay on your device unless you choose an AI feature that uploads audio for processing.

### 1.2 Transcripts, summaries, and AI outputs
- If you request transcription, summarization, or other AI processing, we process the audio you submit and generate text results (for example transcripts, meeting notes, or assistant replies).
- Those results may be stored on your device in the app and/or processed temporarily on our cloud servers while the task runs.

### 1.3 Device and Bluetooth information
- To connect to your ring, Spur uses Bluetooth permission and may process identifiers and status data needed for scanning, pairing, connection state, battery/device status, and file sync.
- We do not use Bluetooth data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

### 1.4 App and technical information (limited)
- Spur may keep local state needed for core features (for example connection session state, local file lists, and processing status).
- When you use cloud features, limited technical metadata needed to run the request (such as file name, task type, timestamps, and diagnostics) may be processed with the upload.
- We do not sell personal information and we do not use your recordings for advertising.

### 1.5 Account information
- You can use Spur as a guest without creating an account.
- If sign-in, membership, or notification features are added later, this policy will be updated before those features collect additional personal data.

### 1.6 Information you send us
- If you email us (for support or privacy requests), we receive the content of your message and your email address so we can respond.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:
- Connect to and sync with your recording ring
- Store and play recordings you keep on your device
- Provide transcription, summarization, and AI assistant features you request
- Operate, secure, and debug the service
- Respond to support and privacy requests
- Comply with law where required

We process audio for AI features **only when you initiate** an action such as transcription, “Process with AI”, or a related assistant request.

---

## 3. Where Processing Happens / International Transfers

- **On your device:** Bluetooth sync, local storage, playback, and browsing recordings/transcripts happen on your iPhone.
- **Cloud processing:** When you request AI features, audio and related task data are sent to Spur cloud services at `https://api.hispurring.com` for speech-to-text and/or large-language-model processing.
- Those services may run on servers in **Mainland China** and/or other regions, and may use third-party speech or AI providers acting on our behalf.
- If you are in the United States or another country outside China, this means submitted audio and derived text may be transferred to and processed in China (or other regions where providers operate).

If you do not want audio uploaded, do not use AI processing features. You can still sync and keep recordings locally on your phone (subject to device storage).

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only with:
- **Service providers** that process audio or text for us (hosting, speech recognition, and AI model providers), solely to provide features you request
- **Authorities or advisors** if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security

Processors may use the data only to provide the requested service, not to sell it.

---

## 5. Retention

- **On your phone:** Recordings and generated text remain until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app (which removes app-local files).
- **In the cloud:** Uploaded audio and related processing data are retained only as needed to complete the task, operate and secure the service, debug failures, and meet legal obligations. We aim not to keep server-side audio longer than necessary for the task lifecycle.

To request deletion of server-side data tied to an upload or support thread, email [leosun7766@163.com](mailto:leosun7766@163.com) with the subject **Privacy Request**.
